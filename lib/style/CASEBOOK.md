# Casebook

phy's finished pieces, newest first: what each is, its idea in one sentence
and a link. It records what has been done, so that the next piece can do
something else (`STYLE.md` §1, §5); nothing in it is a rule. Before calling a
piece finished, state its idea in one sentence: if the sentence also fits the
last piece of the same kind here, think again. The hand, the voice and the
quiet ground stay out of the sentence, since they are meant to repeat.

**References that have served**, each for one thing, never a whole look
(`STYLE.md` §4):
- The Snowman (Raymond Briggs's book and the 1982 film): soft coloured
  pencil throughout, almost no words, and the pace set by Howard Blake's
  score.
- Ernest & Celestine (2012): colour that stops short of the frame's edges,
  so the paper shows round every scene.
- Jon Klassen's hat books (2011–16): eyes without mouths doing all the
  acting, and menace stated flatly.

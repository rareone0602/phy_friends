Every sample is set with font-size-adjust: ex-height 0.42 so x-heights match; real use would tune each size by hand. Title 64px, label 23px, intro 18px. Red notes mark the shortlist.

Verdict: the thin, uneven hands (Reenie Beanie, The Girl Next Door, Coming Soon, Waiting for the Sunrise, Nothing You Could Do) read as graphite. Gochi, Patrick, Caveat, Covered By Your Grace, Nanum, Gaegu, Gamja, Mynerve and Indie Flower read as felt-tip or marker. Klee One / Yomogi are lovely but too tidy. The grain filter sells "pencil" at title size and is too much below ~24px, so use it on the title only.

Each film plays on its own page, and can be saved as a video frame by frame (see the README).

who does what

Every fwiend belongs to someone, and not everyone knows everyone. phy knows them all; any other two are strangers until their owners say otherwise.

  • strangers share a scene, take turns, look at each other and greet each other from a distance;
  • fwiends whose owners know each other may also stand close, pass things and play on one side;
  • touching, competing and teasing need both owners' say-so.

Only phy speaks in words. The others speak in marks (! ? !? … ♪ z), because only their owners know how they talk. The word ‘draft’ in a corner means that some owners haven't yet said yes to films.
